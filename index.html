Official Rules

Fair play, respect, and competition. Read them, follow them, and you'll fit right in.

  • Treat every player and staff member with basic respect.
  • Hate speech, racism, toxicity, and personal attacks have zero place here.
  • Keep the chat clean — no spamming, no pointless drama.
  • Use the correct channels for what you're posting.
  • Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply on top of ours.
  • Play legit. Hacking, cheating, or unfair mods get you removed fast.
  • Glitches and bugs aren't features — don't exploit them.
  • Solo modes mean solo. No secret teaming.
  • Protected areas are protected. Griefing won't be tolerated.
  • PvP happens in designated zones unless an event says otherwise.
  • Staff calls are final. Respect them.
  • Got an issue? Open a ticket — don't start a public argument.
  • Staff abuse gets reported with proof. We take it seriously.
  • Every event drops its own ruleset. Read it, follow it.
  • No under-the-table advantages during tournaments.
  • Disconnections follow official server policy — no re-dos, no exceptions.

Punishments

Severity decides the response. We don't do warnings for the obvious stuff.

1 Warning
2 Temp Mute
3 Temp Ban
4 Perm Ban
Serious violations skip straight to a permanent ban. No warning required.
